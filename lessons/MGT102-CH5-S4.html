EXAM BOX

Various kinds of barriers arise to managing diversity effectively in organizations. Some barriers originate in the person doing the perceiving; others are based on the information and schemas that have built up over time concerning the person being perceived. To overcome these barriers and effectively manage diversity, managers (and other organizational members) must possess or develop certain attitudes and values as well as the skills needed to change other people’s attitudes and values.

Top management’s commitment to diversity is crucial for the success of any diversity-related initiatives. Managers should consciously attempt to be open to other points of view and perspectives, seek them out, and encourage their employees to do the same. Managers and all other members of an organization should strive to avoid making snap judgments about people; rather, judgments should be made only when sufficient and relevant information has been gathered.

Managers can take many steps to manage diversity effectively, an ongoing process that requires frequent monitoring.

الشرح — الخطوة ٣: Increase diversity awareness

طبيعي إن المدير وغيره يشوفون الناس من منظورهم هم، لأن مشاعرهم وأفكارهم ومواقفهم وخبراتهم توجّه إدراكهم وتعاملهم. بس تقدير التنوّع يحتاج إن الناس ينتبهون لمنظورات غيرهم ومواقفهم وخبراتهم.

  2. المنظورات المختلفة للمدراء والموظفين والزملاء والعملاء المتنوّعين (the differing perspectives of diverse managers, employees, coworkers, and customers)

EXAM BOX

Many diversity awareness programs in organizations strive to increase managers’ and workers’ awareness of (1) their own attitudes, biases, and stereotypes and (2) the differing perspectives of diverse managers, employees, coworkers, and customers.

Efforts to increase diversity skills focus on improving how managers and their employees interact with each other and improving their ability to work with different kinds of people.

Managers and their employees must learn how to be open to different approaches and ways of doing things. This does not mean organizational members have to suppress their personal styles. Rather, it means they must be open to, and not feel threatened by, different approaches and perspectives and must have the patience and flexibility to understand and appreciate diverse perspectives.

الفخ
  • Awareness ≠ skills: الوعي = تعرف مواقفك وتحيّزاتك ومنظور غيرك. المهارات = تتعامل وتتواصل فعلياً (أساليب تواصل، لغة، إشارات غير لفظية، سوء فهم). سيناريو فيه «تعابير الوجه ولغة الجسد» أو «حل سوء الفهم» ← skills. سيناريو فيه «تكشف تحيّزك» أو «تحط نفسك مكان غيرك» ← awareness.
  • أهداف برامج الوعي ست. اختيار زي «Eliminating all differences among employees» أو «Teaching employees to adopt the majority culture» = مو منها.
  • Flexibility ما تعني suppress their personal styles — هذا أشهر فخ في الخطوة. المرونة = انفتاح وصبر بدون ما تحس بتهديد.
  • Cafeteria-style benefit plans وflexible work hours وwork from home = أمثلة على المرونة (الخطوة ٥)، مو على المكافأة (الخطوة ٩).
  • «Mentally walk in other people’s shoes» = هدف من أهداف برامج الوعي (رقم ٥) — مو تعريف الإرشاد ولا المرونة.

EXAM BOX

Whenever feasible, it is desirable to rely on objective performance indicators because they are less subject to bias. When objective indicators are not available or are inappropriate, managers should ensure that adequate time and attention are focused on the evaluation of employees’ performance and that evaluators are held accountable for their evaluations. Vague performance standards should be avoided.

If members of certain groups are underrepresented in particular kinds of jobs or units, managers need to understand why this is the case and resolve any problems they uncover.

When managers or employees witness another organizational member being unfairly treated, they should be encouraged to speak up and rectify the situation. Top managers can make this happen by creating an organizational culture that has zero tolerance for discrimination. As part of such a culture, organizational members should feel empowered to challenge discriminatory behavior, whether the behavior is directed at them or they witness it being directed at another employee.

If effective management of diversity is a valued organizational objective, then employees should be rewarded for their contributions to this objective.

EXAM BOX

Many managers use a multipronged approach to increase diversity awareness and skills in their organizations; they use films and printed materials supplemented by experiential exercises to uncover hidden biases and stereotypes. Accurate information and training experiences can debunk stereotypes.

David Thomas, formerly a professor at Harvard Business School and currently the president of Morehouse College in Atlanta, has studied the careers of underrepresented groups in corporate America. One of his major conclusions is that mentoring is very important for underrepresented groups, most of whom have reached high levels in their organizations by having a solid network of mentors and contacts. Mentoring is a process by which an experienced member of an organization (the mentor) provides advice and guidance to a less experienced member (the protégé) and helps the less experienced member learn how to advance in the organization and in their career.

According to Thomas, effective mentoring is more than providing instruction, offering advice, helping build skills, and sharing technical expertise. Of course, these aspects of mentoring are important and necessary. However, equally important is developing a high-quality, close, and supportive relationship with the protégé.

  2. إجراء يستخدمه الموظفين للإبلاغ (a procedure for employees to use to report instances of harassment)
  3. شرح للإجراءات التأديبية اللي تنطبق لو صار تحرّش (disciplinary actions)
  4. التزام بالتثقيف والتدريب عن التحرّش (a commitment to educate and train)
٢Use a fair complaint procedure to investigate charges of sexual harassment. استخدم إجراء شكاوى عادل للتحقيق. الإجراء لازم (أربع شروط):
  1. يديره طرف ثالث محايد (a neutral third party)
  2. يعالج الشكاوى بسرعة وبشمول (promptly and thoroughly)
  3. يحمي الضحايا ويعاملهم بعدل (protect and fairly treat victims)
  4. يضمن إن المتّهمين بالتحرّش (alleged harassers) ينعاملون بعدل بعد
٣When it has been determined that sexual harassment has taken place, take corrective actions as soon as possible. لما يثبت إن التحرّش صار، خذ إجراءات تصحيحية بأسرع وقت. تختلف حسب شدّة التحرّش. ولو كان واسع (extensive)، أو طويل (prolonged)، أو من نوع quid pro quo، أو مرفوض بشدّة بأي شكل ثاني ← الإجراء ممكن يوصل لـفصل المتحرّش (firing the harasser).
٤Provide sexual harassment education and training to all organizational members, including managers. وفّر تثقيف وتدريب عن التحرّش لكل الأعضاء — والمدراء معهم. وأغلب شركات Fortune 500 توفّره الحين لموظفينها.

EXAM BOX

Sexual harassment is not only unethical but also illegal. There are two basic forms of sexual harassment: quid pro quo sexual harassment and hostile work environment sexual harassment.

Quid pro quo sexual harassment occurs when a harasser asks or forces an employee to perform sexual favors to keep a job, receive a promotion, receive a raise, obtain some other work-related opportunity, or avoid receiving negative consequences such as demotion or dismissal.

Hostile work environment sexual harassment can be more subtle. It occurs when organizational members face an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment because of their sex.

Steps that managers can take to eradicate sexual harassment include development and communication of a sexual harassment policy endorsed by top management, use of fair complaint procedures, prompt corrective action when harassment occurs, and sexual harassment training and education.
